Tutorials are **learning-oriented**. They take you by the hand through a first
end-to-end experience so you learn by doing, with a guaranteed result at the
end. They do not try to be complete — once you have done one, the
[how-to guides](../how-to/) and [reference](../reference/) cover the rest.
