How-to guides are **goal-oriented**. Each one assumes you already know the
basics (do a [tutorial](../tutorials/) first if not) and shows the steps to get a
specific job done. They omit background — for that, see
[explanation](../explanation/) — and they are not exhaustive — for every detail,
see [reference](../reference/).
