Explanation is **understanding-oriented**. These pages step back from the keys you
press and discuss how the game is put together and why it works the way it does.
Read them to build a mental model — not to look up a fact (use
[reference](../reference/)) or to get something done (use
[how-to guides](../how-to/)).
